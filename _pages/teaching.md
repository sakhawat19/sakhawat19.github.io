CSC 250 – Foundations of Computer Science I

Graduate Teaching Associate (Instructor of Record)
Spring 2026 · UNC Greensboro

Course introducing foundational concepts in computer science with an emphasis on logical reasoning and discrete structures.

Topics include logic, proof techniques (direct, contrapositive, contradiction, induction), sets, relations, functions, number theory, and introductory graph theory.

Focus on rigorous proof-writing, mathematical reasoning, and algorithmic thinking for computer science majors.
