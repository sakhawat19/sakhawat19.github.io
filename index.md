My research focuses on the **reliability, privacy, and hallucination detection** of Large Language Models (LLMs),
and **Social Network Analysis (SNA)** of Magdalenian hunter-gatherer societies.

## Research interests

- Hallucination detection and mitigation in LLM outputs
- Social Network Analysis, graph modeling and inference
- Full-stack software (ERP), IoT & fog/edge computing
