I am a **Ph.D. student and Research Assistant in Computer Science at the University of North Carolina at Greensboro**, advised by **Dr. Jing Deng**. My research focuses on improving the **reliability, security, privacy, and efficiency of large language models** for trustworthy deployment.

I develop methods for detecting hallucinations, identifying AI-mediated replies in digital communication, and deploying models efficiently on resource-constrained systems. I also study autonomous agent behavior and contribute to interdisciplinary social network analysis research. My research builds on **over nine years of software development experience**.
